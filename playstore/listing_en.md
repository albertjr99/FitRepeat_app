**App name (max 30)**: Fit Repeat: Strength & Cardio

**Full description (max 4000)**:
Simple workouts, with you in control: build and edit your plans your way, train with a timer and automatic rest, and track your progress — no ads.

WORKOUT OF THE DAY, NO HASSLE
• Ready-made 6-day Push/Pull/Legs split with an editable schedule.
• Log weight, reps and RPE for every set, with automatic rest timer between sets.
• Edit, swap, remove or add exercises at any time; execution tips and video links.

BUILD YOUR OWN PLAN
• Ready templates: Full Body 3x, Upper/Lower 4x, PPL + Upper/Lower 5x, PPL 6x, Upper/Lower arms focus.
• Import your plan from a file (txt, csv, md) or by pasting text.
• Plan analysis: sets per muscle, weekly frequency and intensity.

GUIDED CARDIO
• Cardio plans for bike, running, walking and swimming with heart-rate zones, weekly progression and deload weeks, plus pace alerts.

PROGRESS
• Body weight chart, measurements and stats; training and weigh-in reminders.

PRIVACY
• Your data stays on your device. No ads, no trackers. Optional account with password, fingerprint unlock and e-mail backup.

Units in kg/lb and cm/ft-in. Available in English, Português and Español. Light and dark themes.

Notice: this app is an organization tool and does not replace advice from a physician or physical education professional.
